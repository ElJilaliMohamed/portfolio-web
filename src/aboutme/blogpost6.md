A presentation of Enki's very dystopian sci-fi artworks and his graphic novels. He talked a little about his childhood experiences that led him to ideas for his stories. 

I had the opportunity to have a great talk with him and tell him what I thought about his most recent work 'Bug'. We had a very sweet talk and he's a very sympathetic, easy-going person. He really inspired me to continue drawing and telling amazing stories!
