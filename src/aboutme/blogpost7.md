In this book you can find the novels with the next titles in Dutch: Het Proces, Het Slot, Amerika, De Gedaanteverwisseling etc.

I'm currently at the 7th chapter of "Het Proces", to me, very eerie & mysterious about conflicts that happen since the introduction and we slowly start understanding where they come from while also showcasing different characters situation. 

Would like to tell more, but his writing style in this novel is very descriptive of situations and things unfold quite slowly, but you can really feel the tension in the other hand.
